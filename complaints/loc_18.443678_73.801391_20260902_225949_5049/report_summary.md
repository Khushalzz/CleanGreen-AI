### Critical Bio-Safety & Environmental Hazards

> [!CAUTION]
> **ACUTE LIVESTOCK INGESTION & FOOD-CHAIN HAZARD**:
> - **Active Livestock Ingestion**: A stray cow and goat are actively feeding within the dumpsite, consuming decaying organic food remnants intimately tangled with single-use LDPE carry bags and metallized laminates.
> - **Pathology**: Non-biodegradable plastics cannot be digested or passed through the ruminant digestive tract. Ingested plastics form dense masses in the rumen and reticulum, leading to chronic impaction, malnutrition, peritonitis, and death.
> - **Chemical Bioaccumulation**: Plasticizers (phthalates), printing dyes, and toxic heavy metals from contaminated roadside runoff enter the local milk and food chain.
> - **Physical Laceration Risks**: The protruding jagged steel rebar frame, rusted nails from broken plywood cabinetry, and fractured e-waste casing present severe puncture hazards and tetanus risks for both animals and sanitation workers.
